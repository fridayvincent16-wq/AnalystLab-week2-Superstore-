Week 3 – Advanced Business Analysis & Dashboard

Project Overview

This project is a continuation of the Week 2 analysis using the Superstore dataset. Week 3 focuses on advanced business analysis, deeper investigation of business performance, and the development of actionable insights and recommendations.

The analysis builds on the findings from Week 2 and investigates sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional performance, customer segments, discounts, business risks, and opportunities.

Objectives

The objectives of the Week 3 analysis are to:

* Conduct advanced analysis of the Superstore dataset.
* Identify important sales and profitability trends.
* Evaluate product and category performance.
* Identify products with high sales but low profitability.
* Analyze the effect of discounts on profitability.
* Examine regional and customer segment performance.
* Identify key business risks and opportunities.
* Develop actionable business recommendations.
* Apply DAX measures for advanced calculations and performance analysis.
* Document the analytical process and dashboard development.

Key Areas of Analysis

1. Sales Performance

* Total sales
* Sales trends over time
* Monthly and yearly sales performance
* Year-over-year sales growth
* Sales performance by category and sub-category

2. Profitability Analysis

* Total profit
* Profit trends
* Profit by category and sub-category
* Profit by region
* Loss-making products
* Products generating high sales but relatively low profit

3. Product Performance

* Best-performing products
* Poor-performing products
* High-sales/low-profit products
* Category and sub-category performance

4. Regional Performance

* Sales by region
* Profit by region
* Identification of high- and low-performing regions

5. Customer Segment Analysis

* Sales by customer segment
* Profit by customer segment
* Comparison of segment performance

6. Discount Analysis

* Discount levels across products and categories
* Relationship between discounts, sales, and profit
* Identification of products or categories where high discounts may be affecting profitability

Dashboard

The Week 3 dashboard presents the results of the advanced analysis through interactive visualizations and key performance indicators.

The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders quickly understand:

* Overall sales and profitability
* Sales and profit trends
* Product and category performance
* Regional performance
* Customer segment performance
* Discount and profitability relationships
* Areas requiring management attention

DAX Analysis

DAX measures were used to support the advanced analysis and provide dynamic calculations for the dashboard.

Examples include:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Sales Growth
* Profit Growth
* Year-over-Year Performance
* Profitability metrics
* Product performance calculations

Business Insights

The analysis identifies significant patterns in sales, profitability, products, regions, customer segments, and discounting.

These findings are used to highlight areas of strong business performance as well as areas where corrective action may be required.

Business Risks

Key risks identified through the analysis include:

* Low-profit products despite strong sales.
* Loss-making products.
* Excessive discounting affecting profitability.
* Uneven performance across regions and product categories.
* Dependence on specific products or customer segments for revenue.

Business Opportunities

The analysis also highlights opportunities to:

* Improve pricing and discount strategies.
* Focus on high-profit products and categories.
* Review low-performing products.
* Improve performance in weaker regions.
* Increase profitability without necessarily increasing sales volume.
* Use data-driven decision-making to optimize business performance.

Recommendations

Based on the findings, recommendations are provided to improve profitability, optimize product performance, manage discounts effectively, and strengthen overall business performance.
